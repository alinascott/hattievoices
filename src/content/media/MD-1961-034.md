Posted as a “Movietone Moment” on the anniversary of the 1931 Belize hurricane, but the report itself covers Hurricane Hattie in 1961. Its narration estimates that over 15,000 of Belize City's roughly 30,000 people were made homeless, and shows supplies flown in by Britain and the United States.
