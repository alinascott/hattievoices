News 5 marked the 60th anniversary of Hurricane Hattie with this report drawn from the memories of survivors. Its introduction recalls how the storm turned back towards Belize after appearing to head for Cuba, made landfall between the Belize and Stann Creek districts, and hit Belize City hardest.
