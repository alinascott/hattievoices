The writer's family took refuge on the upper floors of the Paslow Building, at the corner of Queen and North Front streets, alongside nearly 200 other people. The piece recalls a window blowing out during the night, the view of a flooded city from the North Front Street verandah the next morning, and the walk home along Queen Street to find the family's Barrack Road house collapsed. It also mentions the Mexican medical teams, and the plane crash that killed five volunteer Mexican doctors on their way to help.

Some figures in the article, including the death toll and the timing of landfall, differ from other accounts. The byline is "Amandala Press" and the writer is not named.
