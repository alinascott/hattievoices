Cleopatra White was the first rural health nurse in the Gales Point and Manatee River area, where she set up the first village council, a model later used across Belize during hurricanes and other emergencies. The article describes her relief work after the 1931 hurricane and during Hurricane Janet (1955) and Hurricane Hattie (1961). She later worked at the Hattieville clinic, retiring in the mid-1960s.
