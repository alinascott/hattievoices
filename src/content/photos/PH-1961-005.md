A page from the same album as the aerial photograph of Belize City. Its newspaper cuttings follow the storm from its approach, reported from Jamaica as "Hattie brings rain", to its arrival ("The Damage Is Devastating") and the medical relief that followed ("Medicos will be welcomed back as guests"), which describes mass typhoid immunisation in Belize and the work of the Field Hygiene Section. The typed card in the centre lists the members of the RAMC Light Field Hygiene Unit.
