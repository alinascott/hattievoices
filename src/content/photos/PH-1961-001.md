Photographed from 30,000 ft during a Royal Air Force reconnaissance flight in the months after Hurricane Hattie. The original printed caption runs along the bottom edge of the print.

It comes from an album documenting the work of the Royal Army Medical Corps Light Field Hygiene Unit (Army School of Health) in British Honduras after the hurricane, now held by Wellcome Collection. The album also contains extracts from *Soldier* magazine and *The British Honduran*.
