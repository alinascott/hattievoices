A six-page report in the February 1962 issue of *Soldier*, the British Army's own magazine, on the Army's part in the relief effort after Hurricane Hattie. Every page is shown below in reading order.

The article describes how men of Z Company, 1st Battalion, The Royal Hampshire Regiment were the first to bring help, and how more than 1,300 troops followed, flown in from Jamaica and Britain. It covers the immunisation of Belize against typhoid, the feeding centres and street patrols, the Worcestershire Regiment's arrival to relieve the Hampshires, and the sappers who restored water, power and the docks and drove the first stakes at the new settlement that became Hattieville. A panel on page 7, “They Sang Through the Horror”, tells how Major S.G.B. Matthews gathered the regiment's families to shelter at the airport camp; his own photographs of the storm's aftermath are in the archive, starting with [PH-1961-006](../PH-1961-006/).

The scans were shared by Jim Titheridge and published on AmbergrisCaye.com in 2012, alongside the photograph [PH-1961-011](../PH-1961-011/). Some of the small print is hard to read at this size.
