One of five photographs taken by Major S.G.B. Matthews of the 1st Battalion, The Royal Hampshire Regiment, on 31 October and 1 November 1961, now in the collection of the Royal Hampshire Regiment Museum in Winchester.

Taken from the air in the hours after the storm, this view shows how few buildings were left standing in this part of the city.
